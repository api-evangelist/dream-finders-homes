Cities deliver walkability, culture, and convenience, while suburbs bring space, quiet, and a more residential feel. Suburbs can give more home for the money, while cities may reduce transportation needs and car costs. Cities bring access to cultural experiences and amenities close by, while suburbs have space, parks, and highly rated school systems.
