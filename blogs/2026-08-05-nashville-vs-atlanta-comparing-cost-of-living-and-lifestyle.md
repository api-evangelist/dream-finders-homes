Nashville and Atlanta both provide growing economies, vibrant communities, and plenty of opportunities for homebuyers. Nashville has a tax advantage with no state income tax, while Atlanta has a wider range of communities and housing options. Atlanta is larger than Nashville, with a bigger metro area, more employment centers, and a broader mix of neighborhoods.
