Renting offers flexibility and lower upfront costs, making it ideal for those who may move soon. Buying builds equity and can provide long-term financial benefits, including potential home appreciation. New construction incentives may help reduce upfront costs through closing cost assistance and mortgage rate buy-downs for qualified buyers.
