If you're an eligible veteran, active-duty service member, or qualifying surviving spouse, you can use a VA loan to buy a new construction home in Fayetteville, NC. VA loans make homeownership more accessible by offering benefits like no down payment for many qualified buyers, competitive interest rates, and no private mortgage insurance (PMI) in most cases. With its proximity to Fort Bragg, strong military community, and growing selection of new homes, Fayetteville is an ideal place to put your VA home loan benefit to work.
