For military personnel and growing families stationed in the Sandhills region, deciding whether to rent vs buy a home is one of the biggest crossroads you’ll face. When you weigh the rent vs own option, it really comes down to where you want your hard-earned money to go. Renting an apartment gives you short-term flexibility during a quick PCS move, but it also means you're building someone else's equity instead of your own.
