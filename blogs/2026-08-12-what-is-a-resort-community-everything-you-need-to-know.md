Resort lifestyle communities provide homes with built-in recreation, connection, and convenience. Popular features include pools, clubhouses, fitness centers, sports courts, and outdoor spaces. Resort style communities create a lifestyle experience beyond a traditional neighborhood and welcome a variety of residents.
